# Command Center Information Architecture

## Primary navigation

1. **Command**
   - What needs attention now?
2. **Operations**
   - Tasks, queues, workload and exceptions.
3. **Clinical**
   - Drafts, reviews, finalization queues and documentation quality.
4. **Compliance**
   - Requirements, evidence, findings, corrective actions and readiness.
5. **Funding**
   - Funding readiness, evidence coverage and open organizational gaps.
6. **Agents**
   - Mr. NOVA and registered agents, health, permissions and activity.
7. **Integrations**
   - EHR adapters, connection health and synchronization.
8. **Audit**
   - Traceability from command to action to outcome.

## Executive question

Every screen should answer:
What happened?
Why does it matter?
What evidence supports it?
What does Mr. NOVA recommend?
What requires human authorization?
What happens next?
