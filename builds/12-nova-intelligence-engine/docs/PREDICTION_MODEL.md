# NOVA Prediction Model

Predictions are forecasts, not facts.

Supported initial prediction classes:

- compliance risk
- evidence expiration risk
- corrective-action delay risk
- operational queue risk
- documentation completion risk
- integration failure risk
- funding-readiness risk

Each prediction must include:
- target
- forecast horizon
- probability/confidence
- supporting evidence
- assumptions
- uncertainty
- recommended response
- model/version provenance

High-impact predictions require human review before consequential action.
