# NOVA Retrieval Policy

Retrieval is governed by least privilege.

## Required filters

- tenant
- subject scope
- role
- purpose
- sensitivity
- Privacy Hold
- evidence status
- source authority

## Retrieval outcomes

AUTHORIZED_CONTEXT
INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT
CONFLICTING_CONTEXT
DENIED_CONTEXT

NOVA must not manufacture missing context.

For patient-sensitive reasoning, assignment and Privacy Hold checks occur before retrieval.
