# Interpretation Model

NOVA converts source text into structured interpretation without replacing the source.

Each interpretation answers:

- What does the source say?
- Who does it apply to?
- What service/location/jurisdiction does it affect?
- When does it become effective?
- What evidence would demonstrate organizational readiness?
- What operational action may be needed?
- What remains uncertain?

Interpretations have status:

DRAFT → REVIEW → VERIFIED

Only VERIFIED interpretations may be used as authoritative organizational guidance inside readiness workflows.

Even VERIFIED interpretations retain the underlying authoritative source.
