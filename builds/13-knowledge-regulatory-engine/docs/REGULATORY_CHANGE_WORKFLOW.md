# Regulatory Change Workflow

1. Discover new or changed authoritative source.
2. Capture source and retrieval metadata.
3. Create new immutable version.
4. Hash and preserve source content.
5. Compare against prior version.
6. Generate change event.
7. Classify change.
8. Generate draft interpretation.
9. Map affected requirements.
10. Identify affected evidence and corrective actions.
11. Route substantive changes for human review.
12. Recalculate readiness where appropriate.
13. Record audit trail.

NOVA does not silently convert a detected regulatory change into an organizational command.
