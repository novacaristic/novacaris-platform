# Evidence Automation Policy

NOVA may automatically:

- identify missing evidence
- generate evidence requests
- classify submitted evidence
- run deterministic validation checks
- detect approaching expiration
- link evidence to likely requirements
- recommend human verification
- trigger re-evaluation after verified evidence arrives

NOVA may not:

- fabricate evidence
- mark ambiguous evidence verified without configured authority
- delete evidence history
- change source content
- declare legal compliance solely from an AI judgment
- bypass human review where policy requires it
