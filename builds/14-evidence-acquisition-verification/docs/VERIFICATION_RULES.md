# Evidence Verification Rules

Verification is evidence-specific.

Examples:

## License
Check:
- issuing authority
- license number/reference
- facility/entity identity
- service type
- effective date
- expiration
- jurisdiction

## Training record
Check:
- employee identity
- training topic
- completion date
- required frequency
- trainer/provider
- certificate/sign-in evidence

## Policy
Check:
- organization
- policy title/version
- approval authority
- effective date
- review date
- required signatures/acknowledgment where applicable

## Critical rule

A document can be authentic yet insufficient to satisfy a particular requirement.

Verification and compliance evaluation remain separate decisions.
