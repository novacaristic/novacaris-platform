# Readiness Gap Model

Gap types:

- missing_evidence
- insufficient_evidence
- expired_evidence
- compliance_failure
- process_failure
- ownership_gap
- overdue_action
- verification_failure
- regulatory_change

Severity:

CRITICAL
HIGH
MEDIUM
LOW

A gap must identify the underlying requirement or finding whenever available.

NOVA should distinguish:
WHAT IS MISSING
from
WHAT IS NON-COMPLIANT
from
WHAT IS UNKNOWN.
