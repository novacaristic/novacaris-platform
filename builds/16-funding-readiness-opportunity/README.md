# Build 16 — NOVA Funding Readiness & Opportunity Engine

Build 16 extends the NovaCarïs readiness system into funding and opportunity intelligence.

## Core loop

OPPORTUNITY → ELIGIBILITY → EVIDENCE → READINESS → GAP → APPLICATION PLAN → REVIEW → SUBMISSION → OUTCOME → LEARNING.

## Opportunity types

- government grants
- foundation grants
- contracts
- procurement opportunities
- payer/provider opportunities
- strategic partnerships
- loans/financing programs
- accelerator/incubator programs

## Critical boundary

NOVA may discover, compare, score readiness for, draft and track opportunities.

NOVA does not guarantee eligibility, funding, award, procurement selection or financing approval.

Final representations, certifications and submissions require the authorization required by the opportunity and organizational policy.
