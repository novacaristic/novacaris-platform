# Eligibility Engine

Eligibility is evaluated in layers:

1. Explicit source eligibility.
2. Organization facts.
3. Evidence supporting those facts.
4. Requirement-by-requirement matching.
5. Unknowns and conflicts.
6. Preliminary eligibility result.

Statuses:

ELIGIBLE
LIKELY_ELIGIBLE
POSSIBLE
INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION
NOT_ELIGIBLE

NOVA must not convert POSSIBLE or LIKELY_ELIGIBLE into a claim of guaranteed eligibility.
