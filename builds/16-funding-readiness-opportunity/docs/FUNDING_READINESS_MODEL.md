# Funding Readiness Model

Funding readiness measures preparedness for a specific opportunity.

Suggested dimensions:

- eligibility evidence
- organizational readiness
- evidence completeness
- narrative readiness
- budget readiness
- required attachments
- partnerships
- reporting capacity
- submission readiness

The score is opportunity-specific.

A high readiness score means the organization appears prepared to submit; it does not predict award probability with certainty.
