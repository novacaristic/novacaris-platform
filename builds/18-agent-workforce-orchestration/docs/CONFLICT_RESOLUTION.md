# Agent Conflict Resolution

Specialists may disagree.

Example:
Compliance NOVA identifies a requirement as high risk while Funding NOVA considers the opportunity feasible.

The system must:
1. preserve both outputs
2. preserve their evidence
3. identify the conflict
4. avoid silently selecting one
5. ask for additional evidence or human decision when material
6. record the final resolution

Mr. NOVA synthesizes; it does not erase disagreement.
