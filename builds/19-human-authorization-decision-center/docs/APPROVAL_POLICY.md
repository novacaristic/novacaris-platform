# Approval Policy

Approval requirements are determined by Build 08 policy.

Examples:

LOW:
single authorized reviewer.

MEDIUM:
authorized owner or designated supervisor.

HIGH:
designated accountable reviewer plus required policy checks.

CRITICAL:
multi-party approval or designated executive/clinical/compliance authority as configured.

The examples are policy patterns, not universal legal requirements.

No approval screen can manufacture authorization by changing a client-side field.
