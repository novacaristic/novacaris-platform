# Initial Monetization Model

NovaCarïs can support a layered commercial model:

## Platform subscription
Core SaaS access.

## Module subscriptions
Compliance, readiness, funding, command center and other modules.

## Usage
Metered services such as NovaClerk voice/documentation or API/integration activity.

## Seats
Per-user or role-based pricing.

## Add-ons
Additional locations, users, usage or specialized services.

## Academy
Training and certification.

## Consultancy
Human-led services supported by NovaCarïs workflows.

Exact prices should be validated against market demand before being hard-coded into the product.
