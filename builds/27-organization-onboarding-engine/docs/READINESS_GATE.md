# Go-Live Readiness Gate

Before activation, NovaCarïs should verify:

- tenant is active
- commercial entitlement is valid
- required organization profile is complete
- required users/roles exist
- enabled services are configured
- required policies exist
- required integrations pass verification
- required evidence is available
- critical readiness blockers are resolved
- required human approval exists

Readiness does not mean regulatory approval, licensure, accreditation, Medicaid enrollment or legal certification.
