# Build 33 — NOVA Consultancy & Professional Services Engine

Build 33 supports the delivery and management of human-led professional services using NovaCarïs workflows.

## Core loop

LEAD → DISCOVERY → SCOPE → PROPOSAL → AGREEMENT → PLAN → DELIVER → REVIEW → ACCEPT → CLOSE → LEARN.

## Scope

- client organizations and contacts
- discovery and needs assessments
- engagement scopes
- proposals and service packages
- milestones and deliverables
- consultant assignments
- task and time tracking
- expenses
- review and acceptance
- change requests
- client communications
- invoicing references
- outcomes and engagement history

## Service examples

- behavioral-health operational readiness
- compliance evidence organization
- policy/procedure development support
- corrective-action planning
- grant-readiness support
- technology implementation
- NovaCarïs Academy training
- workflow optimization

## Professional boundary

NovaCarïs can organize and support consulting work. It does not make unqualified legal, clinical, accounting, licensing or grant-award guarantees. Deliverables requiring licensed professional review must be routed accordingly.

Human consultants remain accountable for professional judgments, representations and client acceptance.
