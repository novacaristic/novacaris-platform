# Professional Boundaries

Consultancy support may include organizing evidence, preparing draft policies, identifying gaps, coordinating implementation and tracking deliverables.

The platform must not represent its output as:
- legal advice unless supplied by an appropriately qualified professional under an applicable engagement
- licensing approval
- guaranteed Medicaid enrollment
- guaranteed grant award
- accreditation
- clinical diagnosis or professional judgment without appropriate qualified review

Regulatory conclusions should cite authoritative sources and preserve uncertainty and review status.
