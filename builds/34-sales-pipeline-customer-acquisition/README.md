# Build 34 — NOVA Sales Pipeline & Customer Acquisition Engine

Build 34 manages the path from prospect discovery to a qualified opportunity, approved proposal and customer engagement.

## Core loop

CAPTURE → ENRICH → QUALIFY → DISCOVER → PROPOSE → FOLLOW UP → NEGOTIATE → WIN/LOSE → HAND OFF → MEASURE.

## Scope

- lead and prospect records
- lead sources and campaigns
- organization/contact relationships
- qualification criteria and scoring
- pipeline stages
- activities and follow-up tasks
- discovery meetings
- proposals and quotes
- opportunity value and forecast
- win/loss reasons
- conversion to Build 33 engagement
- sales metrics and attribution
- notification hooks for follow-up

## Initial go-to-market use cases

- NovaCarïs SaaS subscriptions
- NovaClerk
- NovaCarïs Academy
- compliance/readiness packages
- professional consultancy services
- enterprise implementation

## Governance

Sales scores are prioritization aids, not facts about a prospect. Outreach follows consent, channel rules and privacy requirements. AI may draft communications and recommend next steps, but must not fabricate customer intent, send unauthorized communications, or make binding commercial commitments.
