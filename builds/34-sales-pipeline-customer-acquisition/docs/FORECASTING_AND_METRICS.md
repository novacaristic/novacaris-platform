# Forecasting & Metrics

Useful metrics:
- leads by source
- qualification rate
- discovery-to-proposal rate
- proposal acceptance rate
- sales-cycle duration
- win/loss rate
- pipeline value
- weighted forecast
- campaign cost and attributed conversion
- revenue by product/service
- renewal and expansion opportunities

Forecasts are estimates, not guaranteed revenue. Report actual billed, collected and recognized revenue separately when available.
