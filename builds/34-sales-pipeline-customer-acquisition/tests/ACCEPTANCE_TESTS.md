# Build 34 Acceptance Tests

- [ ] Leads are tenant-scoped.
- [ ] Lead source and campaign attribution can be retained.
- [ ] Qualification criteria are explicit and versioned.
- [ ] Unknown qualification inputs remain unknown.
- [ ] Pipeline stage changes are recorded.
- [ ] Follow-up activities can be scheduled and completed.
- [ ] Outreach eligibility is distinct from follow-up due date.
- [ ] Proposals are versioned.
- [ ] Proposal approval is distinct from customer acceptance.
- [ ] Won opportunity conversion requires approved proposal.
- [ ] Conversion retains source opportunity reference and is idempotent.
- [ ] Sales stage alone cannot activate paid services.
- [ ] Forecasts are identified as estimates.
- [ ] Opt-outs and communication preferences are respected.
- [ ] Sales events are auditable.
