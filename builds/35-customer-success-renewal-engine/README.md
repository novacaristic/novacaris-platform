# Build 35 — NOVA Customer Success & Renewal Engine

Build 35 manages the post-sale customer lifecycle: onboarding follow-through, adoption, value realization, account health, renewals and expansion.

## Core loop

HANDOFF → ACTIVATE → ADOPT → MEASURE VALUE → SUPPORT → REVIEW → RENEW/EXPAND.

## Scope

- customer success accounts
- onboarding and adoption milestones
- success plans and outcomes
- customer health observations
- support/escalation references
- periodic account reviews
- renewal opportunities
- expansion opportunities
- feedback and satisfaction
- churn-risk signals
- customer-success tasks
- renewal reminders
- handoff to Build 34 sales pipeline

## Design principles

- Health scores are explainable signals, not objective truth.
- Missing telemetry must be labeled unknown, not assumed healthy or unhealthy.
- A churn-risk prediction is not a customer decision.
- Renewal outreach follows communication policy and preferences.
- Commercial changes require authorized approval.
- Customer success data must not contain unnecessary clinical or protected health information.
