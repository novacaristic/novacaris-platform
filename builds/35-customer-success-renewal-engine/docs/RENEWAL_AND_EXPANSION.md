# Renewal & Expansion

Renewal and expansion records are opportunities, not commitments.

Workflow:
1. identify renewal window
2. review customer-stated outcomes
3. inspect service status and open issues
4. prepare a review
5. confirm proposed scope and pricing
6. route to Build 34 sales
7. obtain required commercial approval
8. record customer decision

Do not automatically renew, increase charges or change entitlements without the required agreement and authorization.
