# Revenue Metrics

Potential metrics:
- recurring revenue
- annualized recurring revenue
- new bookings
- invoice totals
- cash collected
- refunds and credits
- outstanding receivables
- failed-payment rate
- churn and expansion
- revenue by product/service

Define calculation rules and time period. Bookings, invoiced revenue, cash collected and accounting-recognized revenue are distinct measures. Financial reporting should follow the organization's accounting policy.
