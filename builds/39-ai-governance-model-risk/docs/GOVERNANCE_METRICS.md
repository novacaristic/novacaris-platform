# Governance Metrics

Potential measures:
- percentage of deployed use cases with current risk assessment
- evaluation pass/fail by model version
- open high-severity AI incidents
- mean time to contain and resolve incidents
- deployments with complete approval evidence
- prompt/configuration changes awaiting review
- drift observations by disposition
- rollback frequency
- human override and escalation rates

Define numerator, denominator, reporting window and source for every metric. Metrics are governance signals, not proof of safety or compliance by themselves.
