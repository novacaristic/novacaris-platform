# Monitoring & Drift

Monitor relevant signals such as:
- error and refusal rates
- latency and availability
- unsupported-claim/factuality evaluations
- policy violations
- human override patterns
- distribution changes
- user-reported issues
- subgroup performance where appropriate
- incident and complaint trends

Signals should have thresholds, ownership and escalation paths. Drift alerts prompt investigation; they do not by themselves prove model degradation or causation.
