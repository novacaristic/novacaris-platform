# Use-Case Risk Classification

Classify the use case, not only the model.

Consider:
- impact on health, safety, rights or access to services
- autonomy and external side effects
- sensitivity of data
- reversibility of errors
- scale and affected population
- degree of human oversight
- failure detectability
- applicable contractual and regulatory requirements

Use the highest applicable risk level when uncertainty is material. Prohibited uses remain blocked. Risk classification requires accountable review and a documented rationale.
