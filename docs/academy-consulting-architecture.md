# NovaCarïs Academy & Consulting — Commercial Architecture

## Purpose

NovaCarïs Academy and NovaCarïs Consulting are official commercial layers of the NovaCarïs Behavioral Health Operating System.

They are not separate from the platform. They convert NovaCarïs intelligence into:
- education
- workforce capability
- implementation services
- compliance readiness
- AI transformation
- recurring advisory relationships

**Mr. NOVA is the intelligence layer supporting both.**

---

## 1. NovaCarïs Academy

### Mission

Turn NovaCarïs operating knowledge into structured learning, practical capability, assessments, portfolio evidence, and professional pathways.

### Academy product ladder

1. **Free Intelligence**
   - briefings
   - webinars
   - checklists
   - introductory guides

2. **Core Courses**
   - Behavioral Health Operations
   - AI Without Fear
   - COMAR & Regulatory Readiness
   - MPRIME Readiness
   - Medicaid Operations
   - Clinical Documentation & Evidence
   - AI Governance for Behavioral Health
   - Funding & Grant Readiness

3. **Applied Labs**
   - compliance gap lab
   - evidence-mapping lab
   - MPRIME readiness lab
   - AI-agent governance lab
   - workflow redesign lab
   - funding opportunity lab

4. **Professional Certificates**
   - NovaCarïs Behavioral Health AI Operations
   - NovaCarïs Compliance Readiness
   - NovaCarïs AI Governance
   - NovaCarïs Implementation Specialist
   - NovaCarïs NOVA Administrator

5. **Enterprise Academy**
   - organization-specific training
   - staff cohorts
   - implementation certification
   - annual competency refresh
   - administrator training

### Academy evidence model

Course completion should produce evidence such as:
- enrollment
- attendance/activity
- assessment result
- project submission
- instructor review
- certificate
- competency status
- renewal/expiration where applicable

The Academy should eventually feed qualified learning evidence into the NovaCarïs organizational readiness model.

## 4. Mr. NOVA Integration

Academy and Consulting use the same intelligence foundation.

### Academy

Mr. NOVA:
- recommends learning pathways
- maps competencies to organizational needs
- generates practice scenarios

### Consulting

Mr. NOVA:

- evaluates regulatory applicability

## 7. Trust Boundary

Academy and Consulting do not bypass the NOVA Trust Layer.

Training completion, recommendations, client information, regulatory conclusions, and consequential actions remain subject to appropriate access controls and provenance.

Mr. NOVA may recommend.

Authorized humans remain responsible for consequential organizational and clinical decisions unless a separately approved system boundary explicitly permits an action.
