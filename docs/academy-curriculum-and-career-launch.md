Status: draft for owner review. Updates `docs/academy-consulting-architecture.md`, which lists courses and certificates but not the learning levels, the Career Launch track, the book and cohort products, or the credential wording rules. Last updated 2026-10-26.

## 1. Guiding question

Every Academy course answers one of three questions: Can I use AI? Can I trust it? Can I prove I handled it responsibly?

## 4. Career Launch track

A track layered over every level: role map, portfolio from capstone work, resume bullets (action + what + tool or standard + result), LinkedIn checklist, interview question bank by role, mock interview scorecard, 30-day job search plan. Target roles: AI governance coordinator; quality or risk analyst; AI implementation assistant; clinical documentation lead; junior AI security reviewer; forward deployed engineer trainee.

## 5. Credential and marketing rules

- Academy certificates are certificates of completion. They are not accredited credentials, professional licensure, ISO certification or regulatory approval.
- No placement, salary or outcome guarantees in any marketing.
- Training is not legal advice.
- "Claude Certified Associate" can read as an official Anthropic credential. Use an independence disclaimer (independent study guide, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic) and obtain legal review of the title before launch.
- Do not claim SOC 2 or other certifications that are not held.
- Portfolio items must be free of client names and private data.

## 6. Launch sequence

1. Healthcare AI Governance cohort (ready to launch; founding cohort of 12 seats to test demand).
2. AI Without Fear Parts 1-5 as the beginner entry course.
3. CCA1 sample chapters, then the full book.
4. Builder and FDE material after the first cohort shows what learners need.

## 7. Teaching standard

Every page ends with a "Words to know" footer defining the special terms used on that page. Each session follows one 90-minute shape: welcome, one big idea, live demo, learner activity, failure story, wrap-up.
