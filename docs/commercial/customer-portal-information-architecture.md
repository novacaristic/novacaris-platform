## Overview screen

The first screen should answer five questions immediately:

1. **Where are we?**
2. **What is at risk?**
3. **What is missing?**
4. **What should we do next?**
5. **Where can NovaCarïs help?**

## Visual hierarchy

1. Overall readiness
2. Critical/high findings
3. Action queue
4. Evidence health
5. Domain readiness
6. Academy/Consulting/Software recommendations
7. Assessment history

## Safety UX

Never use language such as:
- "approved"
- "compliant"
- "guaranteed eligible"

unless an authoritative source and appropriate human determination actually establish that state.

Prefer:
- "assessment indicates"
- "evidence present"
- "human review required"
- "verification needed"
- "potentially eligible"
- "action required"

## Customer conversion principle

The portal is simultaneously:
- a product
- a service delivery surface
- an evidence workspace
- a learning gateway
- a software discovery surface

But recommendations must remain evidence-driven and transparent.
