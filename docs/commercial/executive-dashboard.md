# NOVA Executive Readiness Dashboard

## First screen

The Executive Dashboard is the primary customer landing surface.

### Header

**Good morning — [Organization]**

"Mr. NOVA has organized your current readiness picture and highlighted what needs attention."

### Hero

**Overall Readiness**
- score / 100
- status
- assessment date
- rule version

### KPI cards

- Priority findings
- Open actions
- Human review
- Evidence gaps

### Priority Findings

Show the highest-impact items first.

Each item:
- priority
- domain
- status
- title
- action
- human-review indicator

### Action Queue

Show the next actions in execution order.

Each action:
- action title
- originating finding
- status
- authorization requirement

### Recommended Next Steps

Three columns:

**Academy**
Training and competency pathways.

**Consulting**
Services that address demonstrated gaps.

**Software**
NovaCarïs products relevant to identified workflows.

### Safety language

Use:
- "assessment"
- "readiness"
- "evidence gap"
- "human review"
- "verification required"

Avoid:
- "government approved"
- "fully compliant"
- "guaranteed eligible"

unless supported by an authoritative determination.

## UI principle

The dashboard should feel like an operating cockpit, not a marketing homepage.

The customer should understand their situation within seconds and reach the next action within one or two clicks.
