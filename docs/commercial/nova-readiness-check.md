# NOVA Readiness Check — Commercial Product

## Position

**NOVA Readiness Check** is the entry product for NovaCarïs Consulting.

It converts a provider's organization, sites, services, evidence, regulatory obligations, operational state, and funding posture into a structured readiness picture and prioritized action plan.

It is powered by Mr. NOVA but does not replace qualified human review or legal/compliance judgment.

---

## Customer promise

> **Know what applies to your organization, what evidence you have, what is missing, what needs human review, and what to do next.**

The product is designed for behavioral-health organizations preparing to:
- launch
- enroll or revalidate
- expand services
- respond to regulatory change
- prepare for an audit
- improve documentation/evidence
- implement AI
- pursue funding
- scale operations

---

## Product tiers

### 1. NOVA Snapshot

Entry-level diagnostic.

**Best for:** small providers and prospects needing a fast first look.

Outputs:
- organization/site/service intake
- initial readiness indicators
- top gaps
- priority actions
- recommended Academy learning
- recommended next assessment

Commercial model:
- fixed-fee diagnostic
- optional credit toward a larger assessment

### 2. NOVA Readiness Check

Core consulting product.

Outputs:
- Organization → Site → Service map
- applicable regulatory requirements
- evidence inventory
- missing/stale/conflicting evidence
- MPRIME readiness where applicable
- operational readiness findings
- funding-readiness findings
- prioritized corrective-action roadmap
- human-review queue
- executive readiness report

### 3. NOVA Implementation Sprint

Turns the assessment into execution.

Outputs:
- implementation backlog
- owners
- evidence requirements
- target dates
- verification criteria
- Academy training assignments
- software/workflow recommendations
- completion evidence

### 4. NOVA Transformation Program

Long-term engagement.

Combines:
- consulting
- Academy
- NovaCarïs software
- regulatory intelligence
- funding intelligence
- AI governance
- recurring readiness monitoring

### 5. NOVA Enterprise Advisory

Recurring intelligence and transformation relationship.

Focus:
- regulatory change

- AI governance
- funding pipeline
- evidence/audit posture
- operating performance

## Readiness output

The customer receives:

1. **Executive Readiness Score**
2. **Domain-level scores**
3. **RED / AMBER / GREEN priorities**
4. **Evidence gap register**
5. **Regulatory applicability map**
6. **MPRIME status**, when applicable
7. **Funding-readiness view**
8. **AI governance view**, when applicable
9. **Top 10 recommended actions**
10. **Human-review queue**
11. **30/60/90-day roadmap**
12. **Recommended Academy pathway**
13. **Recommended NovaCarïs products/services**

The score is an internal decision-support indicator, not a government certification or guarantee of approval.

## Mr. NOVA workflow

`Intake → Context → Organization/Site/Service → Regulatory assessment → Evidence validation → MPRIME assessment → Funding assessment → AI/governance assessment → Prioritization → Human review → Report → Action plan`

Consequential actions do not bypass the NOVA Trust Layer.

## Intake

Minimum initial intake:

- organization name
- organization type
- jurisdictions
- sites
- services
- payer/program relationships
- current licenses/approvals
- current operational stage
- primary business objective
- known compliance concerns
- funding objective
- technology/AI objective

Evidence can then be added progressively.

---

## Academy routing

Assessment findings can create recommended learning pathways.

Examples:

**AI governance gap**
→ AI Governance for Behavioral Health

**COMAR/evidence gap**
→ COMAR & Regulatory Readiness

**MPRIME operations gap**
→ MPRIME Readiness Lab

**general AI literacy gap**
→ AI Without Fear

Training recommendations are advisory unless an authorized consulting engagement establishes required training.

---

## Consulting routing

Assessment results may recommend:

- NOVA Implementation Sprint

- AI Transformation

## Evidence and trust

Every material assessment finding should be traceable to:
- source
- evidence record
- assessment rule/version
- timestamp
- reviewer, where applicable

Regulatory material must be versioned and status-aware.

Proposed regulatory language must not be represented as current enforceable law.

Funding intelligence is not a guarantee of eligibility or award.
