# Government Opportunity Intelligence

## Scoring model

Eligibility 30%
Mission/population fit 20%
Geography 10%
Capability 10%
Evidence readiness 10%
Activity alignment 10%
Timing 10%

## Recommendation policy

85–100: EXCELLENT / PURSUE
70–84: STRONG / PURSUE
50–69: POSSIBLE / PARTNER
0–49: WEAK / MONITOR

Hard eligibility failure: INELIGIBLE / DO_NOT_PURSUE

## Important behavior

The system should explain:
- why an opportunity scored highly
- what evidence supports the score
- what is missing
- whether a partner could cure an eligibility/capability gap
- whether the deadline is realistic
- what the applicant should do next

A high score must never be interpreted as a grant award prediction.
